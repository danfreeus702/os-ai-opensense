## How to use

1. Open the Apply/search link for a company.
2. Find an entry-level remote role (IT support I, cloud support associate, junior cloud/DevOps).
3. Copy the pitch matching the category in the table.
4. Attach your resume and submit through the official form.
5. Track submissions in your own spreadsheet; follow up after 7-10 days.

Note: roles and links change frequently. If a company has no current entry-level remote opening, skip it rather than applying to an unrelated senior role.
