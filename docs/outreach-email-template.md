# Outreach email template (canonical)

Use this for all future job/cold-outreach letters. Tailor the role phrase and
company name, but always include the forwarding line near the end.

## English

```text
Estimado equipo de {company},

I am writing to apply for an entry-level IT / {role_phrase} opportunity with
{company}. I am based in Hermosillo and I am looking for hands-on work with
networking, servers, and infrastructure.

What I bring:

- Built and documented a native OPNsense AI Security Wizard plugin: 52 commits,
  configd snapshot/undo, plain-language diffs with exact GUI paths, an offline
  help index, and a hardware-aware cloud assistant. Tested on two OPNsense 26.7
  VMs with a 157-link menu sweep and zero console errors.
- Wrote "Networking for Beginners" and "No-Leak DNS for Homelabs", covering
  TCP/IP, subnetting, routing, DNS, Wi-Fi, and a seven-step troubleshooting
  workflow.
- Wrote "Terraform + FLOCI: AWS Labs Without the AWS Bill", with real labs for
  S3, SQS, DynamoDB, SSM, state backends and CI.
- CCNA (Cisco, 2006). Strong on Linux, Proxmox, DNS/DNS-over-TLS and documented
  change management.

Portfolio: https://github.com/danfreeus702/os-ai-opensense

Please forward this email to anyone you think may be interested in talking to me.

Kind regards,
Daniel Freeman
casainspector66@gmail.com
```
